      How your privacy is kept

      • Your files never leave your device. Every conversion runs in this browser tab, in background workers. FileConvert has no server that receives files, and the page is not allowed to send anything to one: its security policy permits connections only back to this site, to load the app's own code, fonts and codecs.
      • No accounts, no ads, no tracking. There is no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics on this page.
      • What the network is used for. Opening the page downloads the app. The first conversion of each kind downloads that converter (for example the HEIC decoder or the PDF fonts), once. After that, conversions keep working without a connection while the tab stays open.
      • Large results stay private on your device. Audio and video results are written to your browser's private storage for this site while you work, and are deleted when you remove them, clear the list, or close the tab. Other results are held in memory.
      • Metadata is left behind. Converted pictures do not carry the original's EXIF details, such as camera information or GPS location.
      • Files are treated as untrusted. Scripts, macros and embedded objects in documents are never run. Size and expansion limits stop files built to exhaust memory.

      The only thing remembered between visits is your theme and conversion settings, in this browser's local storage.
