# Changelog

## v0.2.0 - 2026-11-26

v0.2.0 adds 16 tools to the converter: Merge, Split, Extract, Delete, Reorder and Rotate PDF pages, Images to PDF, PDF to JPG and PNG, Remove PDF metadata and Remove photo metadata, Compress PNG, JPG, WebP and AVIF, Resize images, and Combine images into a TIFF; new formats and conversions (Excel and JSON, TSV, XLS and ODS, JSON Lines, XML and YAML tables, Markdown and Word, HTML to DOCX, SVG, ICO, multi-page TIFF, AIFF, MPEG-TS, animated GIF, WebP and PNG, ZIP, TAR and GZ archives); and a page for each tool and for the most common conversions, 66 in all. Everything still runs in the browser: nothing is uploaded.

### PDF tools

- **Merge PDFs**, a new tool with its own page. PDFs in the order you set become one: every page copied as it is (nothing is redrawn or recompressed), with its links, annotations and form fields; links, bookmarks and named destinations point at the right pages of the merged file. Bookmarks are rebuilt as one per file, named after it, with the file's own bookmarks under it, open or closed as they were. A form field, named destination or form font whose name an earlier file already uses is renamed (name_2 in the second file), so nothing is overwritten, and the default appearance of each file's fields is kept. Layers keep their settings. The first file's title, author and other document information are kept. Each PDF is checked as it is added (pages, a digital signature, the PDF guard); encrypted PDFs are refused, and a signed PDF has to be accepted before merging, since its signature will no longer be valid. Accessibility tags, page labels, files attached to the document, document scripts, article threads and XFA form data are not carried, and the result lists what each file loses. Up to 200 files, 5,000 pages and 1 GB together, within the memory check below.
- **Split a PDF, Extract pages, Delete pages, Reorder pages and Rotate pages**, five new tools with their own pages, on the same checks as Merge PDFs. Split by page ranges (overlapping allowed), every few pages, or into single pages, with names that say the pages (report-p04-07.pdf) and a ZIP for many; split and extract copy only the pages taken, so nothing of the others is in a new file, and keep the links, bookmarks, named destinations and form fields that belong to their pages. Delete leaves nothing of the deleted pages in the file and never deletes every page. Reorder by dragging, with Up and Down, or by typing the order; links and bookmarks follow their pages. Rotate a quarter turn either way or upside down, every page or some, adding to the rotation a page already has. Page lists are typed as 1-3, 7, 10-, and mistakes are refused with what to write instead.
- **Images to PDF**, a new tool with its own page, and pages for JPG to PDF and PNG to PDF. Pictures in the order you set become one PDF, one a page (every page of a multi-page TIFF), on A4, US Letter or pages the size of each picture, portrait, landscape or automatic, with a margin; each picture is fitted whole by default, never enlarged unless you choose it, and filling the page (cutting off edges) is an option whose result says how much was cut. JPEG data is copied byte for byte (no recompression), without its EXIF, GPS, XMP and comments; PNG data goes in as it is where PDF can hold it, 16 bits and palettes included, and is otherwise decoded exactly, transparency kept; EXIF orientation is applied by the page layout, so no pixel is resampled; color profiles and CMYK are kept. HEIC, WebP, AVIF, GIF, BMP and TIFF are decoded once and stored without further loss, and every color conversion and bit depth reduction is reported. Each picture is checked whole as it is added (checksums, complete data, the JPEG end marker) and refused with the reason when damaged; a PNG whose data would expand past its picture is cut to the picture. Its memory is estimated from the headers before anything is decoded, and a set that would not fit is refused before it starts.
- Reverse in Reorder PDF pages works on PDFs of any length up to 5,000 pages. The order is kept as a list, its text writes runs that go down as one range (5000-1), and a typed order may use such ranges too (4234-1, 4235-5000); split, extract, delete and rotate refuse ranges written backwards. Typed page lists are expanded only as far as the PDF has pages.
- When pages are removed, a form's calculation order keeps no field of a removed page, and that field's value goes with it.
- Removing pages counts the entries hidden under a closed bookmark correctly, and keeps each form field's widget and each note's popup with its own page.
- **PDF to JPG and PDF to PNG**, a new tool with a page for each. Every page, or the pages you choose, saved as pictures at 72, 150 or 300 dpi or a resolution of your own (10 to 1,200), each page drawn from its crop box and turned by its rotation, as PDF readers show it, comments and filled-in form fields included. JPG pictures have a white background and the quality you choose; PNG pictures are lossless, with a transparent background as an option. The size of each picture is shown before you start, and nothing is drawn smaller to fit: a page past 100 megapixels (4,096 x 4,096 pixels on a phone or tablet whose browser does not report its memory), or work that needs more memory than the browser can give, is refused with the resolution that fits. Pages are drawn by pdf.js in a worker, one at a time, and the pictures wait in the browser's private storage until you download them, one by one or as a ZIP; names carry the page number, padded so they sort (report-p007.png). Every PDF goes through the PDF guard first, and encrypted PDFs are refused.
- **Page previews in the PDF tools.** Split, extract, delete, reorder and rotate, and PDF to JPG and PNG, show small pictures of the PDF's pages. Choose pages by clicking them (Shift-click for a run of pages) or with the arrow keys and Space, with Select all and Clear selection; the typed page list and the previews follow each other. Reorder shows the pages as pictures to drag or move with Up and Down, rotate shows the chosen pages turned, and split shows the file each page goes to. Previews are drawn in a worker of their own, only for pages near the screen, at most 160 kept, and a 5,000-page PDF is still one light tile per page. A PDF too large to preview on the device gets numbered tiles and the reason, and no tool needs a preview to run. The browser frees what previews used late, so a tool counts it; where the device has memory for the tool or for its previews but not both, the previews stop, saying so, and the tool runs.
- **Remove PDF metadata**, a new tool with its own page. It removes the document information (title, author, subject, keywords, creator, producer, dates and custom fields), XMP metadata of the document and of its pages, pictures and fonts, application data stored with pages, page thumbnails, web capture information, and EXIF, GPS, XMP, IPTC and comments inside JPEG pictures without touching their picture data; the file is written whole with a new identifier made from its own bytes, so earlier versions kept inside it do not survive. Pages, comments, bookmarks, links, form fields, attachments and layers stay, and the result lists which of them the PDF has, since they can name people too: removing metadata does not make a document anonymous. A PDF/A or PDF/UA file no longer conforms. Signed PDFs ask first.
- PDF text in Helvetica or Arial that a PDF names without including the font is drawn with Arimo 1.31 (Apache License 2.0) instead of the Liberation Sans files pdf.js ships (GPL 2.0 with a font exception). Arimo has the same design and character widths: compared page by page, text is placed on the same pixels, and the pictures differ from before by 0.16 of 255 on average.
- **First pages in Merge PDFs.** Each file in the list shows a small picture of its first page, drawn in the background one file at a time, only for files near the screen. A file too large to draw on the device, or a browser that cannot draw in the background, keeps a plain document tile with the reason, and merging never waits for a picture.
- Where a browser cannot draw in the background (no OffscreenCanvas), PDF to JPG and PNG says so when its page opens and takes no file, and the catalog marks it as not available in this browser, instead of refusing only after a file is added.
- Page sizes start at the usual paper for the browser's language and region (US Letter in the United States, Canada, Mexico, the Philippines and the other countries the Unicode CLDR lists for it; A4 elsewhere and when no region is set), read from the browser's language settings only. In Images to PDF, a page size you choose is remembered in this browser and used next time.

### Image tools

- **Compress PNG**, a new tool with its own page. PNG files made smaller without changing a pixel: oxipng chooses filters and compression for each picture, and may store it as a palette, in gray or with fewer bits where every pixel stays the same. Each result is checked against its original pixel by pixel (red, green, blue and alpha at 16 bits, every frame and its timing for an animated PNG) before it is offered. A color picture with an RGB color profile stays color, since a gray PNG cannot carry that profile, and 16-bit pictures stay 16-bit unless you allow 8 bits where every value fits. Effort Standard, Thorough or Most; metadata kept unless you choose to remove it.
- **Compress JPG**, a new tool with its own page (it is the page for JPEG too). Lossless: the compressed picture written again with Huffman tables made for it, as jpegtran does, baseline or progressive, every pixel the same in every viewer and checked by reading the result back. At a quality, or to a size in KB or MB: decoded and compressed again with MozJPEG, the quality searched from 10 to 95 for the highest that fits, the dimensions never changed for it; making the pictures smaller is a separate option. EXIF with the orientation, the color profile, XMP, comments and the pictures after the main one (an HDR gain map, a depth map, a motion photo, with their Multi-Picture index corrected) are kept unless you choose to remove metadata. CMYK photos become sRGB. SSIM can be shown for each result.
- **Compress WebP** and **Compress AVIF**, new tools with their own pages. WebP: lossy at a quality or a size, or lossless with every pixel kept and checked; by default lossless files stay lossless. AVIF: lossy at a quality or a size, or lossless where AVIF can keep every pixel, at the file's own 8, 10 or 12 bits, with its color description (HDR PQ and HLG, Display P3) and color profile kept, and its rotation, mirroring and crop applied to the pixels. Transparency is kept; animated WebP and AVIF are refused with the reason rather than losing their animation.
- **Resize images**, a new tool with its own page: JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF and BMP to fit within a width and height (the shape kept, by default), to an exact size (stretched, only when chosen) or by a percentage, in batches. Nothing is enlarged unless you allow it and nothing is cropped. Each picture is turned upright from its EXIF orientation and scaled with Lanczos-3, transparency weighted so edges do not darken; 16-bit PNGs stay 16-bit. Save in the same format or as JPG, PNG, WebP or AVIF; the color profile, and unless removed EXIF and XMP (and a JPG's comment into a JPG, a PNG's text into a PNG), go with it, and each result lists what it kept and what its format could not hold.
- Every compression result says its size before and after, the bytes and the share saved, its dimensions, whether it is lossless or lossy and what changed, and a compression that would not make a file smaller gives back the original unchanged, with the reason. **Compare with the original** lays a lossy result over its original, with a slider to move between them. Each picture's memory is estimated from its header and work that would need more than the browser can give is refused before it starts.
- **Remove photo metadata**, a new privacy tool with its own page. It takes EXIF camera details, dates, GPS location, maker notes, embedded thumbnails, XMP, IPTC and Photoshop data, comments, PNG text fields and extra data after the picture (such as a motion photo's video) out of JPG, PNG and WebP photos, without recompressing them: the picture data is copied byte for byte. Orientation (as a minimal EXIF block holding nothing else) and the color profile are kept unless you turn them off. Each photo says what was removed and kept and its size before and after; batches download as a ZIP.
- **Combine images into a TIFF**, a new tool: pictures in the order you set become the pages of one multi-page TIFF, each turned upright, uncompressed or LZW; a multi-page TIFF among them adds all its pages.

### Conversions and formats

- **Excel to JSON and JSON to Excel.** JSON from a workbook keeps numbers, true/false and empty cells as JSON values and writes dates as ISO 8601 text; pick one sheet or get all of them in one file. JSON to Excel gives typed cells, one sheet per list, text that can never run as a formula, and every digit of large integers. New pages: Excel to JSON and JSON to Excel.
- **TSV is its own format**, read and written, to and from CSV, JSON and Excel, with the same formula protection as CSV. Converted files say how many cells protection changed, and it can be turned off.
- **AIFF and AIFF-C** audio converts to every audio format: 8 to 32-bit, little-endian, float, mu-law and A-law, read in pieces however large; 16-bit AIFF to WAV or FLAC keeps every sample. Compressed AIFF-C is refused with its codec named. New page: AIFF to MP3.
- **MPEG-TS** (.ts, .mts, .m2ts) converts like other video. Its stream table is read, so what cannot be converted is named instead of dropped: MPEG-2 video (TV recordings) offers only its sound, and sound in a private stream (AVCHD camcorders) is reported. New page: MPEG-TS to MP4.
- **Animation is kept.** Animated GIF, WebP and PNG (APNG) convert to animated GIF, animated WebP, MP4 or WebM with every frame, its timing, the loop count, transparency, and the disposal and blending rules of the source. The output menu marks which formats keep the animation and which keep only the first frame, and says so before converting. New pages: GIF to WebP and WebP to GIF.
- When a browser cannot encode H.264 at a size, an animation converted to MP4 now says it holds VP9 instead.
- **SVG to PNG, JPG, WebP and the other picture formats**, drawn at its own size, two or four times larger, or a width up to 4096 pixels. Every SVG is cleaned first: scripts, event handlers, HTML content, animation and every link to a file, font or stylesheet outside the SVG are removed, entity declarations are refused, and nothing in the file runs or is downloaded. New pages: SVG to PNG and SVG to JPG.
- **ICO in and out.** Icons from any picture: a favicon with 16, 32 and 48 pixel sizes, all six sizes up to 256, or one size, each kept transparent. ICO and CUR files are read too, whether their pictures are PNG or 32-bit, 8-bit or 1-bit bitmaps with a transparency mask. New page: PNG to ICO.
- **Multi-page TIFF.** Every page of a multi-page TIFF converts (one file per page, or one TIFF keeping them all in order), or only the first page if you choose. TIFF can be written with LZW compression, which is lossless and read by Windows, macOS and Office.
- **Unpack ZIP, TAR and GZ archives**, .tar.gz and .tgz included. An archive gets Extract files instead of a list of outputs; its files are listed with what each one is, to download one by one, or to add to the list and convert without adding them again. Archives are never converted into another archive format. Limits apply to what is actually unpacked, not to what the archive claims: a file that unpacks to more than its listed size or expands more than 1000 times is stopped, and the number of files (10,000) and the total unpacked size (4 GB) are capped. Paths that climb out of the archive, absolute paths and drive letters are shown without those parts and never used; links, devices, encrypted files and unsupported compression methods are listed with the reason; an archive inside an archive is unpacked only when you ask; ZIP and gzip checksums are verified. Unpacked files are kept in the browser's private storage and deleted once nothing in the list needs them. RAR and 7-Zip are not supported, and search says so.
- **Markdown and Word.** HTML to Markdown, DOCX to HTML, DOCX to Markdown and Markdown to DOCX. Markdown output is GitHub-flavored, with tables, fenced code with its language, nested lists and footnotes; DOCX to HTML is one standalone page that loads nothing; Markdown to DOCX uses Word's own heading, list, link and table styles, so the navigation pane and a table of contents work. Code and quote styles from Word, LibreOffice and pandoc, and text set in a code font, become code and quotes. Everything is sanitized first, a document cannot choose its own HTML, and nothing is fetched: pictures a DOCX only links to are left out with a note, and pictures from the web stay addresses or become their description. These conversions keep the structure, not the look, and say what they leave out. A new Documents setting chooses whether pictures stay inside the Markdown. New pages: HTML to Markdown, DOCX to HTML, DOCX to Markdown and Markdown to DOCX.
- **HTML to DOCX**, with its own page. The page is cleaned as for every other output and written in Word's own styles: headings, real Word lists that keep their starting number, tables with their header row, merged cells and tables inside cells, links (links within the page become jumps to the heading they name), code, quotes, underline, highlighting, subscript and superscript, and the PNG, JPEG, GIF and BMP pictures inside the file. Styles are not applied, and pictures from the web or a relative address become their description; nothing is downloaded.
- **Markdown with its pictures as separate files.** A third choice for pictures in Markdown (Documents settings) gives one ZIP for DOCX or HTML to Markdown: the `.md` file with an `assets` folder beside it, picture names that need no escaping, a picture used twice stored once, and SVG pictures cleaned before they are written. The links are relative, so they work wherever the ZIP is extracted, a GitHub repository included. Keeping pictures inside the Markdown is still the default, and a document with no pictures inside it still gives the `.md` alone.
- **XLS and ODS workbooks** convert to XLSX (every sheet, value, formula with its saved result, number format and merged range, and for XLS the column widths and hidden sheets), CSV, TSV, JSON and JSON Lines, with the sheet choice XLSX has. What is not kept (cell formatting, charts, pictures, comments, macros) is listed. New pages: XLS to XLSX, XLS to CSV, ODS to XLSX and ODS to CSV.
- **JSON Lines** (`.jsonl`, `.ndjson`, and NDJSON saved as `.json`, recognized by its lines) to and from JSON, CSV, TSV and Excel. A line that is not valid stops the conversion and is named rather than skipped. New pages: JSON Lines to CSV and JSON to JSON Lines.
- **XML and YAML to CSV, TSV and Excel**, by written rules (README, How data becomes a table): XML records are the elements that repeat, one table each; JSON and YAML lists of records are tables; what sits beside the records is repeated on every row instead of being dropped. JSON to CSV and Excel follow the same rules, so an object holding a list of records beside other fields now gives one row per record instead of a single row. Two lists of records give two tables, never a combination of their rows; lists of records inside a row stay in that row; a list that mixes records with other values is kept whole; and the notes explain each of these. New pages: XML to CSV, XML to XLSX and YAML to CSV.

### Privacy and safety

- **PDFs are checked before they are read.** PDF to TXT now runs every file through a guard first, which measures compressed data and the sizes readers allocate from without trusting what the file declares, and refuses files built to make a reader use gigabytes of memory (decompression bombs in content, fonts, pictures and object streams, through Flate, LZW, RunLength, ASCII85 and ASCIIHex, chained and with predictors; pictures, sampled functions and rows of absurd size; too many objects or pages; Brotli streams; forms drawn inside forms so often that no device could read them). Measured on the previous build, such files used up to 4 GB before failing with a misleading message. Whether a page is a scan is now decided without decoding its pictures.
- **Encrypted PDFs are not opened**, including ones that open without a password but restrict copying or printing; until now their text could be extracted. FileConvert never removes a PDF's protection.
- **PDF work is checked for memory before it starts.** Every PDF tool and PDF to TXT estimates the memory a task needs from what the guard counts in each file (its size, the bytes read as objects, pages, the largest stream, the page content and embedded fonts once decoded, how much content one page holds, how much content and how many fonts forms make the reader go through again, forms inside forms, soft masks, patterns and appearances that many annotations share included, the soft masks one page sets, the largest picture; for a split, what the new files hold together) and refuses the task, with the estimate and the allowance, when it is more than the browser can give: half the memory the browser reports for the device, or 2 GB on a computer and 1 GB on a phone or tablet where it reports none, 4 GB at most, and within the JavaScript heap limit Chrome and Edge report. Measured before this check, merging two PDFs of 20,000 form fields each on a small heap made Chrome stop responding with no way to cancel; a 40 KB PDF made the editing library hold 932 MB; and splitting a 4 MB PDF whose 300 pages share one picture into single pages took 2.5 GB. Each is now refused as the file is added or before anything is copied. The figures were measured in Chrome, Firefox and WebKit, then checked against files made for the purpose and not used to fit them; where those needed more, the figures were raised and checked again on further unseen files. Merge PDFs no longer allows 128 MB per gigabyte of reported memory; the estimate replaces it.
- The guard reads each picture's header within the picture itself, so PDFs with thousands of small JPEG pictures (a 209 MB manual with 9,268 of them, in a test) are read rather than refused as too much work.
- THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt also carries the license of the Adobe character maps pdf.js uses for Chinese, Japanese and Korean text.

### Fixes to what v0.1.2 did

- The batch ZIP deflates text results with the browser's own zlib. The compression library it used before (fflate 0.8.3, streaming) was found to write invalid data for some binary input; no text tried was affected, but the same code was underneath.
- A tab left open while FileConvert is updated, which then asks for a converter the new version no longer has, now says to reload the page instead of blaming memory.
- Video whose sound this browser cannot decode (AC-3 outside Edge, for example) now converts without sound, as the note on the file says; it used to stop with an error unless Remove the sound was on.
- Video and audio lengths are measured from the first timestamp, so files that do not start at zero show their real length.
- Emoji and other characters beyond the Basic Multilingual Plane are kept in workbooks written by Python tools such as pandas; they used to become a different character on the way to CSV.
- Mediabunny and its encoders are updated to 1.66.2.
- JSON, JSON Lines and YAML to CSV and Excel no longer lose a value when a key with a dot in its name (`a.b`) sits beside a nested field of the same dotted name: the later one is `a.b_2`. Empty lists and objects are written `[]` and `{}` with either list setting (numbered columns used to leave them empty), a top-level list beside the records follows the list setting (it was always spread into numbered columns), and an empty list, an empty object or records without fields give an empty file with a note instead of blank lines. A key repeated inside one JSON object, which every JSON reader resolves to its last value, is now counted in a note instead of passing silently.
- Spreadsheet to CSV with values as shown no longer cuts numbers in the General format to 11 characters (123456789012345 used to become 1.23457E+14, and 1234.5678901234 became 1234.56789); they keep the 15 digits Excel stores.

## v0.1.0 - 2026-11-15

The first release.

### Conversions

- **Images:** JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, HEIC/HEIF, BMP, GIF and TIFF to JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF, BMP and TIFF, with quality, resizing (by percent or to fit a box), transparency kept or replaced by a chosen color, and EXIF orientation applied. Animated GIF to animated GIF, MP4 or WebM.
- **Audio:** MP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC, M4A and OGG to MP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC, M4A and OGG (Opus), with bitrate, sample rate and channels, and the soundtrack of any supported video.
- **Video:** MP4, WebM, MOV and MKV to MP4, WebM, MOV, MKV and GIF, with resolution, quality and removing the sound. Streams are copied without re-encoding when the new container can hold them.
- **Documents:** TXT to PDF, PDF to TXT, Markdown to HTML and PDF, HTML to PDF, and DOCX to TXT.
- **Data:** JSON to CSV, YAML and XML; CSV to JSON and XLSX; YAML to JSON; XML to JSON; XLSX to CSV.

### Interface

- Drag and drop or a file picker, many files at once, format detection from file contents with a note when the name is wrong, outputs offered per file, Set all to, per-category settings, a queue with real progress, cancel, Convert all, single downloads and a batch ZIP, numbered names when results share a name.
- Dark and light themes, keyboard use throughout, a layout that works from 360 pixels wide, Share, and Back to RadMage Labs.

### Privacy and security

- Every conversion runs in the browser; nothing is uploaded, and the Content Security Policy allows connections only to the site itself.
- Content-based detection, decompression bomb checks for images and ZIP-based documents, refusal of XML entity declarations and YAML alias bombs, nesting limits, HTML sanitizing, spreadsheet formula protection in CSV, safe file names, and size limits that keep the browser from running out of memory.
- Pictures lose their EXIF metadata. Audio and video keep descriptive tags but not raw container metadata.
